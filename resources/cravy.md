# Brand & Vision

Define the core identity, mission, and visual language of CRAVY.

- Mission & Values
    
    Our mission is to help people discover restaurants that match their personal taste, turning every meal into an adventure.
    
    **Core Values:**
    
    - Authenticity — Real opinions, real experiences
    - Discovery — Every day is a chance to find something new
    - Community — Food brings people together
- Brand Manifesto
    
    Why CRAVY exists: Because choosing where to eat shouldn't be overwhelming. We turn cravings into missions and meals into memories.
    
- Tone of Voice
    
    **Playful, relatable, and empowering.**
    
    - Use casual, friendly language
    - Celebrate small wins ("You crushed that burger mission!")
    - Avoid corporate jargon — speak like a friend
- Visual Identity
    
    **Primary Colors:** Orange (#FF6900)
    
    **Text Colors:** #000000, #1D293D, #62748E
    
    **Background Colors:** #FFFFFF, #CAD5E2
    
    **Fonts:** Poppins
    
    **Style:** Modern, vibrant, approachable
    
- Taglines & Copywriting Library
    - "Find your next craving."
    - "Your taste, your mission."
    - "Discover. Rate. Repeat."
    - "Where food meets adventure."

# Product Strategy

Understand the market, users, and positioning of CRAVY.

- Target Personas
    
    **Persona 1**
    
    - Age: 16-30
    - Loves trying new places and sharing experiences
    - Active on Instagram and TikTok
    
    **Persona 2**
    
    - Age: 28-40
    - Wants quick, reliable recommendations
    - Values efficiency and quality
- Market Research
    
    **Competitors:** Yelp, Google Maps, TheFork, Zomato
    
    **Positioning:** CRAVY is not just a review app — it's a gamified discovery platform that makes food exploration personal and fun.
    
- Problem/Solution Statement
    
    **Problem:** Too many restaurant options, generic reviews, decision fatigue.
    
    **Solution:** CRAVY creates personalized "taste missions" (Cravies) that guide users to discover places aligned with their preferences.
    
- Core Features List
    - Cravies — Personalized taste missions
    - Restaurant ratings & reviews
    - Map-based discovery
    - Crews — Social groups for shared missions
    - Badges & gamification
    - Photo uploads & stories
- Naming Rationale
    
    **Why "Cravy"?**
    
    Short, memorable, and derived from "craving" — it's active, personal, and embodies the excitement of discovering your next favorite spot.
